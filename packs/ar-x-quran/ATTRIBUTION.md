# Attribution and licensing — `packs/ar-x-quran`

This pack is **derived data**, and its provenance is not the same as the other packs'. Nothing here
comes from Leipzig, so the Leipzig licence confirmation does not apply.

⚠️ **THIS FILE DID NOT EXIST UNTIL 2026-08-04.** It was written because
a pack check started failing any pack that had a `frequency.txt` and no attribution — a
check added after `packs/ar` was found shipping without one. The gate caught this pack in the same
run it was written for, which is the argument for the gate.

## `frequency.txt` — derived by counting, not downloaded

⚠️ **IT IS DERIVED, NOT AUTHORED, and hand-editing it breaks the pack silently.** It is generated by
running this pack's own `key()` over the mushaf and ranking by corpus frequency
(`npm run vocab:write`, proven by `src/corpus.test.ts`). Before 2026-08-02 the two disagreed: 1,252 keys the
tokenizer produced were absent from the vocabulary, and since new units are introduced FROM the
vocabulary, **10.3% of the Qur'an was permanently unlearnable** while every test passed.

Counts over a text are facts about it rather than a copy of it, and no verse is redistributed here —
`frequency.txt` is word forms and ranks.

## `lemmas.tsv` — Wikidata Lexemes

Surface forms lemmatized against Wikidata's Arabic lexemes by `builders/build_lemmas_wikidata.py`, plus
2,689 mushaf (Uthmani) spellings inverted from the build's `uthmani.tsv`.

> Wikidata. <https://www.wikidata.org/>

**Licence: CC0.** Public domain dedication; no attribution obligation. Named here anyway.

⚠️ **THIS SECTION WAS MISSING UNTIL 2026-09-13**, and `src/attribution.test.ts` is what noticed:
the file described the text and the counts and never said where the lemma table came from.

## The underlying text

The Qur'anic text itself is not under copyright: it is a seventh-century work, and no modern rights
attach to the words.

**A particular DIGITISATION is a different question.** The verse text used to build this pack was
fetched from:

> `semarketir/quranjson` — <https://github.com/semarketir/quranjson>

⚠️ **THE TERMS OF THAT REPOSITORY HAVE NOT BEEN VERIFIED HERE, and "the Qur'an is public domain" is
not an answer to the question.** A digitiser can attach terms to their edition — the encoding, the
verse splitting, the diacritics — even where the words are free. This is the same trap that
`docs/adr/0013` records for translations: the underlying work being old says nothing about the
edition somebody typed.

What reduces the exposure, and what does not:

- **Reduces it:** this pack ships no verse text. `frequency.txt` is counts; `lemmas.tsv` is a
  surface-to-lemma table. `sample.txt` **is** running text — check its provenance before treating it
  as covered.
- **Does not reduce it:** the passages in Supabase are verse text and are a separate question from
  this pack.

**Action owed:** confirm `semarketir/quranjson`'s licence, or re-derive from a source whose terms are
stated — Tanzil publishes its editions with explicit terms. Until then this is a known unknown that
is written down rather than assumed away.

## `lemmas.tsv`

Surface forms mapped to lemmas, built on `luraty-language-packs`. Morphological analysis of a
public-domain text; no third-party lexicon is redistributed.

## Deliberately absent: translations

⚠️ **NO VERSE TRANSLATION SHIPS, AND THAT IS A DECISION RATHER THAN AN OMISSION**
(ADR-0013 in the Luraty app repository). Every widely-used
English translation is in copyright, and several are NC-licensed — which does not help, because
**the Qur'anic track being free forever does not make NC material usable: a free feature inside a
paid app can still be commercial use.** Word-by-word glosses replace it and remove the dependency
rather than negotiating it.

## The track is free forever

Founder, standing: *"I don't want to make any money off of people learning Qur'anic Arabic."* This is
a product commitment, not a licence term, and it does **not** relax any obligation on this page —
see the NC note above for exactly why the two do not cancel out.
