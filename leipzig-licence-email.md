# English version

**To:** wort@informatik.uni-leipzig.de
**Subject:** Licence of the downloadable corpora: CC BY 4.0?

---

Dear Wortschatz Leipzig team,

I am building open frequency lists from your corpora — word forms with counts only, no sentences and
no source text. I am using `deu_news_2024_300K`, `deu-de_web_2021_300K`, `ara_news_2020_1M` and
`ara_news_2022_1M`, and intend to publish under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and a citation of
Goldhahn, Eckart & Quasthoff (LREC 2012), including for commercial use.

Two short questions:

1. Your Terms of Usage say "All corpora provided for download are licensed under CC BY." The
   hyperlink points to version 4.0; the text gives no version. Is **CC BY 4.0** correct?

2. `repo.data.saw-leipzig.de` lists Wortschatz corpora as **CC BY-NC**. The four above are not among
   them — does CC BY still apply to the download archives?

A brief confirmation would be very helpful; I would keep it alongside the published dataset.

Thank you and best regards,
Youniss Kandah
